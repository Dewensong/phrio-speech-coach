# Code of conduct

Phrio is a learning tool and its community should be a safe place to learn,
question assumptions, and improve work without personal judgment.

## Our standards

We expect contributors to:

- communicate with empathy, specificity, and respect;
- critique ideas and evidence rather than people or identities;
- welcome different levels of experience and language fluency;
- protect private recordings, transcripts, credentials, and customer material;
- acknowledge uncertainty and distinguish automated, fixture, and real-device
  evidence.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
privacy violations, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's
private reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled
in proportion to their impact, which may include a warning, content removal,
temporary restriction, or permanent removal from the project.

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when someone is
officially representing Phrio.
